Our Story

Fifteen years of making strangers feel like regulars.

Grand Hotel opened in 2010 in a beautifully restored early-1900s trading house, three blocks from the river. We kept the original brickwork and timber beams, and built a hotel around them that feels less like a chain and more like a well-loved home — one with excellent linens.

Every room is designed by our in-house team, every breakfast pastry is baked before sunrise, and every member of our staff is trained to remember your name by the second morning. We're independently owned, deeply local, and quietly obsessed with getting the details right.
